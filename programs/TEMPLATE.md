# Program N: Name

> **Status:** Proposed | Approved (date, board vote) | In progress | Done | Stopped
> **Estimate:** ___ hours in total, before donated time
> **Budget cap:** $___ (not to exceed without returning to the board)
> **Builds it:** name (terms in a separate engagement if paid)

## The ask

What the board is asked to approve, in two sentences.

## The problem

What is broken or missing today, and who it affects.

## What gets built

What exists already, and what this program adds.

## Out of scope

What this program deliberately does not do.

## Milestones

Phases in order, each with what is live when it ends. No dates or week counts.

## Done when

The checkable conditions that close the program.

## Cost, ownership and maintenance

Estimated hours, the cap, running cost, who owns it, how it is maintained and reported.

## Open decisions

Questions the board or staff must answer.

## Updates

Dated entries: what shipped, hours billed and donated, spend against the cap.
